From farm to jar

What happens before it reaches you

Morning collection

Milk is collected from two farms outside Ahmedabad before sunrise and reaches our kitchen the same day.

Set and churned

Curd sets overnight and is hand-churned in small batches, the way bilona ghee has always been made.

Slow cooked

Ghee is simmered over a low flame, paneer is pressed light, and sweets are stirred by hand until they're ready.

Packed same day

Everything is jarred or boxed the day it's made and reaches your door within 24 hours.

Our story

Started in one kitchen, still run the same way

DairyLa began with a single copper vessel and milk from two farms just outside Ahmedabad. We haven't scaled the way most dairies do. We still churn ghee by hand, still press paneer in small batches, and still stir halwa until it's ready rather than until the clock says so.

Every jar and box that leaves our kitchen was made that same day, for someone we expect to notice the difference.
